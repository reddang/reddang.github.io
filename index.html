How I work

From business goal to measured outcome

  1. 01

    Discover

    Talk to merchants, users and business owners, and map how the work really happens today (BPMN, use cases).

  2. 02

    Define

    Turn strategy into a BRD and PRD, with user stories and acceptance criteria traced back to business requirements.

  3. 03

    Prototype with AI

    Build clickable prototypes with AI so stakeholders react to something real before engineering starts.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Agile delivery with BA, Engineering, Design, QA and Operations, and UAT before every release.

  5. 05

    Measure

    Track what matters: active users, retention, transactions and task time.
